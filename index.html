Swell privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

The short version

  • Swell has no accounts. You don't give us your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress is saved on your own device.
  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase to see how the game is played. This data is not tied to your name.
  • The game currently shows no ads and has no real-money purchases.
  • We don't sell your data.

Who we are

Swell is a mobile puzzle game made by Nimbosus Games ("we", "us"). You can reach us at nimbosusgames@gmail.com.

What we collect

Saved on your device. The game stores your level progress, stars, in-game gems, chosen color palette, best scores, daily reward streak and the date you last played. This is kept in the app's local storage on your device. We do not receive it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Analytics. We use Google Analytics for Firebase. It records gameplay events such as:

  • the app being opened
  • a level starting or finishing, with the level number, score, stars earned, and how many objects were placed or popped
  • a daily reward being claimed, with your streak length
  • a color palette being unlocked

The Firebase SDK also collects some technical information automatically, such as an app instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, language, and an approximate location (country or region) based on your IP address.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos or microphone data.

How we use it

We use analytics to understand how people play, find bugs, and balance the difficulty of the levels. We do not use it to build advertising profiles.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. Analytics data is processed by Google as our service provider. How Google handles it is described in the Google Privacy Policy and on the Firebase privacy page. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

Ads and purchases

Swell currently shows no ads and has no real-money purchases. Gems are earned by playing and have no cash value. If we add ads or purchases in the future, we will update this policy and the app's store listing before that version is released.

Children

Swell is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will look into it.

How long we keep data

Data saved on your device stays there until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Analytics data is kept for a limited period according to our Firebase retention settings.

Your choices and rights

You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the app, and you can erase your saved progress by clearing the app's data in your device settings.

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Türkiye), you may have the right to ask for access to your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to its processing. Write to us at the address above. Because analytics data is not linked to your name, we may ask you for details that help us find your records, and in some cases we may not be able to identify them.

Security

Analytics data is sent from the app over an encrypted connection (TLS).

International transfers

Google may process analytics data on servers in countries other than your own.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.

Questions? Email nimbosusgames@gmail.com.
